# /pitch-deck -- Hackathon & Investor Pitch Deck

Build a complete 11-slide pitch deck outline with talking points, key messages, and content for each slide. Optimized for hackathon submissions and early-stage investor pitches.

## Slide Structure

1. **Cover** — Product name, tagline, team, event
2. **Problem** — Pain point with data/stats, who suffers, why now
3. **Solution** — What you built, core insight, one-line value prop
4. **Target User** — ICP, persona, market size (TAM/SAM/SOM)
5. **How It Works** — Architecture / user flow, 3-step walkthrough
6. **AI & Technology** — Tech stack, AI/LLM usage, what makes it possible now
7. **Key Features** — 3-5 features tied to user pain points
8. **Prototype / Demo** — Screenshots, demo flow, proof it works
9. **Impact & Value** — Metrics, research backing, why it matters
10. **Future Development** — Roadmap, next 3-6 months, stretch goals
11. **Team** — Who you are, relevant background, why you'll win

## Instructions

Given the product description, for each slide produce:
- **Headline** (one punchy sentence)
- **3-5 bullet points** of content/talking points
- **Visual suggestion** (what to show on screen)
- **Data or quote** to include if available

Use the product's research papers and domain knowledge to ground Impact & AI Technology slides.
Keep each slide to one clear idea. No walls of text.

After generating the outline, suggest which slides need the most work and what evidence to gather.
